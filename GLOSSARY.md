# Laufend Entdecken episode production

How a finished episode of the Laufend Entdecken running podcast gets from a cut video to YouTube, the podcast feed, the website and social media.

## Language

### People

**Host**:
One of the two people who make the podcast and produce its episodes.
_Avoid_: user, cutter, editor

### Episode

**Episode**:
One release of the podcast, identified by its episode number.
_Avoid_: show, production, folge (in code)

**Episode number**:
The sequential number of an episode; it is also the episode's address on the website (`/371`).
_Avoid_: episode id

**Zencastr recording**:
An episode recorded in one Zencastr session; its transcript comes from Zencastr.
_Avoid_: remote recording, zencastr episode

**Interview compilation**:
An episode with interviews cut in from other recordings; its transcript is made from the finished audio.
_Avoid_: interview episode, mixed episode

**Interview**:
An episode in which the hosts talk with a guest, regardless of how it was recorded.
_Avoid_: guest episode

**Host conversation**:
An episode in which only the hosts talk, without a guest.
_Avoid_: solo episode, Host-Gespräch (in code)

**Finished video**:
The fully cut video of an episode, the starting point of production.
_Avoid_: raw video, recording, input file

**Transcript**:
The full text of what is said in an episode, used as the source for all written content.

### Written content

**Shownotes**:
The episode's description on the website: topics, guest and mentioned links.
_Avoid_: description, blog post

**Social posts**:
One post per social platform announcing the episode, posted by hand.
_Avoid_: social media content, teasers

**SEO texts**:
Search and platform metadata for an episode: website title and meta description, focus keywords, Facebook preview texts, YouTube title, description and tags.
_Avoid_: content file, metadata

**Fact-check**:
Verification of names, dates, results and links in the shownotes and social posts against online sources, with a report of what was corrected and what needs a human look.
_Avoid_: verification, editorial research

**Review**:
The step where a host reads and edits the written content and decides images and release date before anything goes public.
_Avoid_: approval, check

### Images

**Cover**:
The square episode artwork shown in podcast players and apps and used for the YouTube video.
_Avoid_: poster, episode image, thumbnail

**Featured image**:
The image shown above the episode's post on the website.
_Avoid_: image, Beitragsbild (in code), wordpress image

### Going public

**Publish**:
The host's decision after the review that hands the episode to YouTube, the podcast feed and the website.
_Avoid_: release, go live

**Release date**:
The date and time at which the episode's website post goes public.
_Avoid_: publish date, scheduled date

**Scheduled**:
An episode that has been published and is waiting for its release date.
_Avoid_: published, done

**Live**:
An episode whose release date has passed, so its website post is public.
_Avoid_: published, released
